Node types:
- Person
- Org
- Object (required attributes: kind)
- Place
- Coordinate (required attributes: lat, lon)
- Event (required attributes: kind)

Edge types (source -> target):
- EMPLOYED_BY: Person -> Org. Person works for Org in a non-executive role; attributes: role, start, end
- EXECUTIVE_OF: Person -> Org. Person holds an executive title at Org (CEO, CFO, managing director) or chairs its board or supervisory board (title chairman, vice chairman, honorary chairman); attributes: title, start, end
- BOARD_MEMBER_OF: Person -> Org. Person sits on the board of Org as a member; a board chair is EXECUTIVE_OF instead; attributes: start, end
- MEMBER_OF: Person -> Org. Person is a member of Org (club, party, association); attributes: start, end
- FAMILY_OF: Person -> Person. Family tie; kind is what the target is to the source: spouse, parent, child or sibling; attributes: kind
- ASSOCIATE_OF: Person -> Person. Persons described as associates, partners or close contacts
- MET_WITH: Person -> Person. Two persons met in person; attributes: date
- COMMUNICATED_WITH: Person -> Person. Two persons communicated (call, email, message, letter); attributes: date, channel
- LOCATED_IN: Object|Org|Person|Place -> Place. Source is or was resident or situated in the Place (dated if the text says when), or a Place lies within another; not a birthplace, not a market; an Org that is based in a Place is HEADQUARTERED_IN; attributes: start, end
- BORN_IN: Person -> Place. Person was born in the Place; attributes: date
- OPERATES_IN: Org -> Place. Org has operations, offices, plants, stores or sales in the Place; attributes: start, end
- HEADQUARTERED_IN: Org -> Place. Org has its headquarters in, or is based in, the Place
- FOUNDED: Org|Person -> Org. Person or Org founded or co-founded the Org; when Orgs merge to form a new Org, each merging Org FOUNDED it; attributes: date
- OWNS_STAKE_IN: Org|Person -> Org. Person or Org owns shares in, a stake in, or controls through ownership the Org; share is the stated fraction; attributes: share, start, end
- SUBSIDIARY_OF: Org -> Org. Source Org is a subsidiary, division or brand of the target Org; publications, magazines, newspapers, imprints and product brands are Orgs linked this way, never Objects; in a chain of owners, link only to the nearest parent the text states; attributes: start, end
- ACQUIRED: Org|Person -> Org. Person or Org bought or took over the target Org; only when the text names the buyer; attributes: date
- HAS_COORDINATE: Place -> Coordinate. Place has the stated numeric coordinate
- OWNS_OBJECT: Org|Person -> Object. Person or Org owns the Object (a physical thing: vehicle, vessel, building, artwork); brands and publications are Orgs; attributes: start, end
- TRANSFERRED_OBJECT: Event -> Object. The Event transferred the Object (sale, delivery, seizure)
- PARTICIPATED_IN: Org|Person -> Event. Person or Org took part in the Event; role is buyer, seller, host, attendee; attributes: role
- HELD_AT: Event -> Place. The Event took place at the Place

Modality of every relation: asserted, negated, hedged, reported.
